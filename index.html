Menu

Evening View / Rooftop Options

Cheapest useful options first. Deals change, so confirm before ordering.

Lumina Rooftop Bar by White Shades

Best cheap rooftop deal: Sunset Hour daily 5–7pm — draught beer from S$10, highball S$11, tap cocktails S$13.

Near Telok Ayer. Good for sunset / CBD rooftop views.

Southbridge Rooftop

80 Boat Quay, Level 5. Excellent river / skyline view. Happy-hour prices are best Mon–Thu; Friday/Saturday use it mainly for the view.

Classic cocktails start around S$18; entrance is from South Bridge Road near Elgin Bridge.

LeVeL33

Marina Bay Financial Centre, Level 33. House-brewed beer with a strong Marina Bay view; generally pricier than Lumina.
