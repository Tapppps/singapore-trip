Singapore Trip App V80

Thursday arrival alternative added.

If you are tired after reaching Hotel Boss:
- Tap Add-ons.
- Choose "Use easy local evening".
- Route becomes:
  Airport -> T4 shuttle -> Changi MRT -> Hotel Boss -> Singapore Zam Zam dinner -> short Kampong Glam walk -> Hotel Boss.
- The easy walk covers the nearby Sultan Mosque / Bussorah Street / Arab Street / Haji Lane area rather than travelling to Marina Bay.
- "Use Marina Bay evening" restores the main Thursday plan.

Map also includes nearby easy-evening dots around Hotel Boss.
